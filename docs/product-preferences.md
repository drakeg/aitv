# Entertainment-first discovery

StreamHub discovery should prioritize watchable entertainment over informational programming.

## Default behavior

- News and current-affairs programming is hidden from discovery rows by default.
- Users can explicitly include news when desired.
- Live-source cards expose genre and network/service information whenever the upstream source provides it.
- Discovery can be narrowed to entertainment genres such as Comedy, Crime, Drama, Reality, Science Fiction, Action, and Documentary.
- Provider/watch actions remain primary; metadata destinations remain secondary.

This filtering applies to discovery results, not to the user's saved/local catalog. Saved items are never silently deleted because of discovery filtering.
